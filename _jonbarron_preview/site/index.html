Publications & Presentations

“DART: From Attentive Reasoning to Intuitive Execution for Experience-Driven Robot Intelligence”

Seungyeop Lee*, Yeeun Kim*, Sooho Park, Seong Oh Lee, Jong Jin Park†

ICRA 2027 · Under review / PDF

Spatial AI / Scene Graphs / LLM & VLM

DART retains task-acquired knowledge in a scene graph for later object-search and navigation tasks. In the Real-Home evaluation (20 tasks repeated over five iterations), it achieved 87.0% overall task success versus 66.0% for DART-Frozen, with 49.3% fewer VLM calls for visual acquisition and description generation.

“A Development of Intuitive Controller and Monocular Vision based Interface for Disaster Response UAV”

Seungyeop Lee

Advisor: Prof. Shinsuk Park

THESIS · Korea University, 2025 / PDF

UAV Teleoperation / Human-Robot Interaction / Monocular Vision

Designed an intuitive UAV controller and a monocular-vision interface integrating 3D reconstruction, obstacle highlighting, and predicted flight paths. In a six-participant simulation study, interface usability increased from 62.5 to 80 on SUS and NASA-TLX mental demand decreased from 57.5 to 32.5.

“Micro UAV Autonomous Navigation System With Deep Learning based Monocular Vision Depth Estimation”

Seungyeop Lee, Shinsuk Park

ICROS 2024 Poster presentation. / PDF

Monocular Depth Estimation / Obstacle Avoidance / UAVs

Used MiDaS relative-depth maps and grid-based direction selection for UAV obstacle avoidance. In PX4/Gazebo simulation, depth-map generation ran at 41 FPS and the reported 10 m flight avoided the placed obstacles.

“Development of ROS based indoor autonomous flight Quadcopter Platform using Visual Odometry”

Seungyeop Lee, Shinsuk Park

ICROS 2023 Poster presentation. / PDF

Visual Odometry / ROS / UAVs

Built a ROS/MAVROS platform that feeds ZED 2i stereo visual odometry to PX4 for indoor localization without GPS. Integrated the physical hardware and demonstrated position-command flight in Gazebo simulation.

“Domain-Transferred Synthetic Data Generation for Improving Monocular Depth Estimation”

Knut Peterson*†, Seungyeop Lee*, Solmaz Arezoomandan, David Han

ICCAS 2025 / PDF

Monocular Depth Estimation / CycleGAN / Synthetic Data

Compared original and CycleGAN-translated synthetic training data across three depth models on NYU-Depth V2. AbsRel improved slightly; other metrics were mixed. CycleGAN training included NYU test RGB images.
