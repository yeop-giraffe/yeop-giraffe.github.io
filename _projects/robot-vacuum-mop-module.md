## Overview

Worked on the mechanism design and production engineering of robotic vacuum cleaner mop modules at Samsung Electronics. The work covered the full path from mechanism prototyping and durability improvement to product validation and production-line quality control.

**Period:** Feb.2025 – Present<br>
**Context:** Full-time engineer, Samsung Electronics<br>
**Product:** VR90F robotic vacuum cleaner

---

## Problem

A production mop module must detect attachment reliably, withstand repeated operation, and remain dimensionally consistent at scale. Meeting these requirements involves both mechanical design and inspection methods that can identify variation during mass production.

---

## Approach

### Attachment Detection

- Prototyped a mechanism for detecting mop attachment and detachment
- Improved detection performance by introducing a shielding plate

### Durability and Validation

- Designed structures to improve module durability
- Developed evaluation methods for measuring mechanism performance

### Production Engineering

- Supported the VR90F from design completion through mass-production readiness
- Managed manufacturability and product-quality considerations during production

### Vision Inspection

- Developed and implemented a production-line vision inspection system
- Used dimensional inspection to improve part consistency and quality control

---

## Outcome

The project connected mechanism design with launch and production requirements, contributing to VR90F mass-production readiness and a more systematic method for monitoring component dimensions on the production line.
