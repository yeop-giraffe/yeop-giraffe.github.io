## Overview

Developed a lightweight autonomous flying vehicle for the Defend the Republic competition at Lehigh University. The vehicle used helium buoyancy to offset its weight and was designed to collect and transport objects during the competition mission.

**Period:** Sep.2024 – Nov.2024<br>
**Context:** Defend the Republic Autonomous Drone Competition, Lehigh University<br>
**Responsibility:** Main body and object-grabbing mechanism

---

## Problem

The vehicle needed enough lift and controllability to move through the competition environment while carrying an object-collection mechanism. Every structural and mechanism choice therefore had to balance stiffness, mass, and ease of fabrication.

---

## Approach

### Buoyant Airframe

- Used helium-gas buoyancy as the primary method for reducing the vehicle's effective weight
- Built a lightweight body from carbon pipes, balsa wood, carbon rods, and vinyl film

### Propulsion Integration

- Integrated small BLDC motors into the lightweight airframe
- Arranged the structure to retain rigidity without adding unnecessary mass

### Object-Grabbing Mechanism

- Designed and built the device used to collect and transport mission objects
- Used 3D-printed parts to create lightweight, task-specific mechanism components

---

## Vehicle Platform

**Lift:** Helium gas buoyancy<br>
**Structure:** Carbon pipe, carbon rod, and balsa wood<br>
**Propulsion:** Small BLDC motors<br>
**Fabrication:** 3D-printed parts and vinyl film
